Professional series · Nitori Taiwan · since October 2025

Shipped at Nitori

Feature pages for Nitori Taiwan's online store, nitori-net.tw: the UX, the visual design and the front-end code. Six are live today, open to try. Three were seasonal campaigns for Mother's Day, Father's Day and Mid-Autumn, shown with their results.

    Seasonal campaign

    Mother's Day 2026

    溫馨5月 · 母親節暖心選品
    Problem
    A gift campaign with nothing on sale, so the page needed a reason to buy beyond price.
    Idea
    Sell a feeling, not a price: one mother's evening in timed scenes, where a NT$15,000 recliner and a NT$300 mug share one story.
    Outcome
    NT$1.17M in four weeks without leaning on discounts. The story-led format went on to shape the next two campaigns.

    Father's Day 2026

    給爸爸的專屬躺平時光 · 父親節放鬆好禮推薦
    Problem
    The same hero recliner, for buyers who weigh function over feeling, across a range spanning rest and work.
    Idea
    Frame his whole day as three chapters, home, desk, lights out, with colour shifting by the hour.
    Outcome
    NT$1.67M in 17 days, 40% above Mother's Day. The lesson: the story draws people in, side-by-side specs close the sale.

    Mid-Autumn BBQ 2026

    中秋烤肉，備料更輕鬆
    Problem
    Barbecue season, told through a homeware range: everyday storage and kitchen pieces with no obvious link to the grill.
    Idea
    Move the story from the grill to the prep: six steps, each opening on a familiar kitchen mishap and its fix.
    Outcome
    The best conversion of the three, 1.19%. Everyday items sell once each one has a moment to solve.

    Electric sofa guide

    電動可躺式沙發 在家享受頂級坐感
    Problem
    A sofa costing tens of thousands, sold from a static page: one image per model, little reason to buy online.
    Idea
    Bring the showroom to the screen: a viewer that reclines each sofa and shows its size, with a fabric-matched care kit offered as an add-on.
    Outcome
    First care-kit sale within a month, after three years without one. Priced against the sofa, the add-on stops feeling like a second purchase.

    Sofa guide

    多款風格沙發 | 打造理想居家空間
    Problem
    The electric-sofa approach, for a range three to four times larger: too many types to weigh at once.
    Idea
    Start from the room: a floor plan and household narrow the range, so the sofas that suit lead and the rest reads as a ranking.
    Outcome
    The lesson of scale: narrow the choice before persuading. The viewer sells the sofa; the shortlist gets them there.
