Professional series · Nitori Taiwan · since October 2025

Shipped at Nitori

I design and build the feature pages of Nitori Taiwan's online store, nitori-net.tw: the UX, the visual design and the front-end code. Six of these pages are live today, so you can open them and try them. The other three were seasonal campaigns for Mother's Day, Father's Day and Mid-Autumn, shown here with their results.

    Mother's Day 2026

    溫馨5月 · 母親節暖心選品

    Instead of a price-sorted grid, I staged the products as scenes in one mother's evening, so a NT$15,000 recliner and a NT$300 mug could share one story. Almost nothing on it was discounted.

    Father's Day 2026

    給爸爸的專屬躺平時光 · 父親節放鬆好禮推薦

    I built the page around three times in a father's day: 19:00 home and reclining, 9:00 back at the desk, 23:00 lights out. The colours shift from daylight to night blue as you scroll, with an electric recliner at the centre. It brought in 40% more revenue than Mother's Day, in 17 days instead of four weeks.

    Mid-Autumn BBQ 2026

    中秋烤肉，備料更輕鬆

    The grilling takes two hours; the prep takes a day. So the page walks through the six prep steps, from portioning to clean-up, each opening with a familiar kitchen mishap and the item that fixes it. A moon in the step bar fills as you go and is full at the last step. It converted best of the three campaigns.
